Daily game and practice

The daily game is the same for everyone and counts in your statistics: games played, wins, current and longest streak. Share your result as a row of pointing hands, without spoilers.

Practice games are the random game and any day from the archive. Play as many as you like: they keep their own guesses and never change the daily ones.

Tips

  • Look at the interface: a verb bar means SCUMM-era LucasArts, a text parser suggests early Sierra, an inventory ribbon a 90s adventure.
  • The resolution and palette give the decade away: 16-colour EGA, 256-colour VGA, high-resolution 2D or 3D.
  • The art style often points to the studio: Revolution's comic lines, Daedalic's painted backgrounds, Wadjet Eye's low-res pixel art.
  • Still unsure? Use a guess: the next screenshot is usually easier.
