What is Point & Clickle?

A free daily puzzle for adventure game fans, inspired by Wordle and Framed. Every day you have six tries to name a point & click adventure game from its screenshots.

When does a new game come out?

Every day at 00:00 UTC. The countdown on the game page shows how long is left.

Which games can come up?

Point & click adventures from the 1980s to today: Monkey Island, Day of the Tentacle, King's Quest, Broken Sword, Discworld, Gabriel Knight, Syberia, Deponia, Blackwell, Thimbleweed Park and thousands more, from the Adventure Gamers database.

Can I play past games?

Yes. The archive has every past daily game, and the random game button gives you a new one whenever you like. Those games are practice: they do not change your daily statistics or streak.

Do I need an account?

No. Your guesses, streak and statistics are saved in your browser only.

Where can I learn more about adventure games?

In our sister site, the Point and Click Museum: a pixel-art museum with the history of the genre, its studios and engines, minigames and classic DOS games you can play in the browser.
